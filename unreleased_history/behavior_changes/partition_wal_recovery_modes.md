With a `partition_wal_usage` other than `kNone`, a WAL record lost while later records survive (a hole in the WAL index order, in one WAL file or across several with `wal_partitions` > 1) is now handled per recovery mode: `kSkipAnyCorruptedRecords` skips the hole and applies the records above it, as it skips unreadable records without WAL indexes, and still never applies records an earlier recovery discarded; `kPointInTimeRecovery` opens the DB with the records below the hole; `kTolerateCorruptedTailRecords` and `kAbsoluteConsistency` refuse to open it. Previously `kSkipAnyCorruptedRecords` also refused to open on such a hole.
