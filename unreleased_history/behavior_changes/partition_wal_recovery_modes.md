In a WAL set written with a `partition_wal_usage` other than `kNone`, recovery merges the WAL files into one stream ordered by their WAL index, and each `WALRecoveryMode` now treats a gap in that stream like N=1 recovery treats a gap in one WAL: `kSkipAnyCorruptedRecords` skips an unexplained hole (a missing, torn, or corrupted WAL index spanned by no surviving explanation) and applies the records above it, `kTolerateCorruptedTailRecords` and `kAbsoluteConsistency` refuse to open when a hole has records above it, and `kPointInTimeRecovery` opens the prefix below the hole. Superseded records are never replayed in any mode. Previously, skip mode also refused to open on an unexplained hole.
