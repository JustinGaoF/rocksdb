With `wal_partitions > 1`, a sync write now becomes visible only once its record and every lower-sequence record are fsynced, as with one partition. A write ordered after a pending sync write becomes visible only after that sync's fsync, and async members of a sync-led write group take the group's lower sequences and return without waiting for the fsync. If the sync fails, the write returns the error and is never visible: not before `Resume()`, not after it, and not after a reopen that follows `Resume()`; every write ordered after it that has not returned fails with the background error, and the DB stops until `Resume()`. Previously the sync write was published before its fsync, and a failed sync was hidden from later reads by a fence and a read mask. A reopen without `Resume()` may recover the landed failed record, as with one partition; the same holds for backups and checkpoints taken before `Resume()`.
