With `wal_partitions` > 1, a write group's WAL append and memtable insert now overlap: the parallel memtable writers start once the group's sequences are assigned, before the append returns, and the group publishes only after both finish. Sync writers are inserted before their fsync and published only once durable, and every group finishes its work before the watermark wait, which gates only the acknowledgment. Merge batches take the concurrent path like any other batch (Open requires `max_successive_merges == 0`); lone-writer groups and N=1 keep upstream's order.
