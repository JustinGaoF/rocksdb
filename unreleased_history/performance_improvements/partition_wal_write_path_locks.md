With `wal_partitions` > 1, a write group crosses partitions only to issue its WAL index and sequence and for the watermark wait: no memtable-switch quiesce when the WAL switch or flush scheduling would not act, per-partition DB-stat and WAL-size shards on private cache lines, no DB mutex at the end of a sync group without a waiter, no all-partition lock in the gate sync, no global mark mutex held across waits, and per-partition recoverable-state slots, which raises multi-partition write throughput. Single-partition behavior is unchanged.
